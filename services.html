FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

What types of exterior cleaning services do you offer?

+

We offer window cleaning, pressure washing, soft washing, gutter cleaning, roof cleaning, and patio & deck cleaning for both residential and commercial properties.

How often should I have my house pressure washed?

+

We recommend pressure washing at least annually to help prevent dirt, algae, and mold buildup, keeping your property looking its best and protecting its surfaces.

Is soft washing better than pressure washing?

+

Soft washing is better suited to delicate surfaces such as siding and roofs where high pressure could cause damage. Pressure washing is better for hard surfaces such as concrete and driveways.

Do you use eco-friendly cleaning products?

+

Yes, we use environmentally friendly, biodegradable cleaning solutions that are safe for your family, pets, and surrounding plants.
